Perspective

Will AI take my job? Hard no.

Our team applies a three-bucket framework (inspired by LinkedIn's CEO) to every project, every week — sorting what AI owns, what AI assists, and what stays human.

AI handles fully

Reports, data pulls, trend scanning, formatting — the mechanical work that used to eat hours. Fully automated.

AI assists, humans review

Weekly leadership reports, benchmarking, analysis — AI drafts it, Mel and I review and validate before it ships.

Human only

Relationships, trust, community building, advocacy cultivation — if you're in customer marketing, AI is never replacing this.

peerbound

The Build

The Canva Advocacy Copilot

A custom GPT in ChatGPT Pro. I upload data weekly from Guru, Gong, Peerbound, Google & Canva files, plus our company goals. It reads everything and produces a leadership-ready report.

What it does

  • Generates weekly reports with metrics, regional trends, and next-step actions
  • Audits our Visionaries program for enterprise adoption gaps
  • Benchmarks us against top peer advocacy programs
  • Surfaces industry trends and what they mean for us

What it replaced

  • Hours of manual spreadsheet processing
  • Scattered data with no single view
  • No benchmarking at all — we were flying blind

It started because I thought: "There has to be an easier way for this to just get itself out."

The Stack

Built tools and bought tools coexist

I don't have time to build everything. I'm a customer marketer — I want to spend the majority of my time on the human aspect of things. Each tool has a lane.

Analyze

Gong

The analyzing tool. What are customers actually saying in conversations?

Activate

Peerbound

Our activation layer. Gets customer proof across teams so they can self-serve. We teach them to fish.

Augment

Custom GPTs

My advocacy copilot for weekly reports. Plus a Customer Proof Assistant on Claude built by my colleague Debbi — sources approved proof and maps champions to campaigns.

Our SDRs use the Customer Proof Assistant to surface proof from Guru. Sales lives in the Peerbound Slack bot. Different tools, different workflows — they complement each other.

Takeaway

My copilot started as
"I don't want to do spreadsheets."

Now leadership depends on it every Thursday. The whole thing runs on a $20/month ChatGPT Pro subscription.

1

Find the task you dread every week

For me it was pulling data from five tools and formatting spreadsheets. What is it for you?

2

Build something rough, then let it earn trust

My first version was bad. But it was faster than doing it manually, so I kept iterating.

3

Don't build what you can buy. Don't buy what you can build.

I build copilots for things specific to my workflow. I buy Peerbound for the things that need to work across the whole org.

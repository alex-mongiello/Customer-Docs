@Wynn Tanner love the question and that's the last part of our flywheel! Find --> Create --> Deliver --> Measure --> Optimize. Optimize is where we give you moments from sales calls where reps should have used customer proof but it wasn't available, and then we give you that intel as well as recommended customers you can get that proof from. **What makes this work:** Explains complex product strategy in plain terms. Acknowledges difficulty honestly. Gives a realistic timeline. Enthusiastic but not overselling.

## Content / Thought Leadership
### Example: Opening of Customer Proof Guide
Customer proof is feedback from real customers that validates your product's value, builds trust, and accelerates sales cycles. Unlike traditional marketing claims, customer proof includes testimonials, case studies, reference calls, reviews, and customer quotes that come directly from users, not your marketing or sales team. In fact, customer proof has become the most trusted form of B2B marketing content, outranking vendor messaging, analyst reports, and paid advertising.
For B2B SaaS companies, customer proof has become essential as buyers increasingly distrust vendor claims and seek peer validation. According to a Gartner survey of 3,500 software buyers, social proof influences 90% of buyers comparing products, with customer reviews being the most influential source (41%) when creating a vendor shortlist.
**What makes this work:** Defines the term clearly without condescension. Cites specific data with sources. Positions the topic in revenue terms. Peerbound isn't mentioned until later, not in the opening.

## Internal Communications
### Example: Core Values Announcement
Here are our Core Values. I am excited about the clarity this list has already given me in terms of what our culture stands for, what behaviors we value, how we intend to operate, and what are the main characteristics in the people we want joining us on our mission.
1. Dazzle Customers: Obsess over creating 'wow' moments for the people we serve.
2. Deliver Results: Set big goals, stay focused, and make it happen.
3. Demand Excellence: Pursue greatness without guilt and bring out the best in everyone.
On a related note, this was a valuable lesson for me on knowing when to push back against AI and the importance of sticking to my own convictions on what will lead to the success of our company. Despite all the hype, AI today works best when it is guided by a skilled person who has a clear, firm vision of the end result. When we cede control and simply 'accept' what AI gives us as gospel (which is all too tempting...), we end up with mediocre output that "feels right" but doesn't get us there.
**What makes this work:** Frames values in terms of practical clarity, not abstract principles. Connects to his AI philosophy naturally. Honest about the temptation to over-rely on AI.

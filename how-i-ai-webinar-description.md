# How I AI

We're all using AI at this point. But here's what most of us won't admit: there's a gap between the AI tools we're paying for and the ones we're actually getting value from.

This webinar is three customer marketing leaders taking 15 minutes each to show you exactly what they use, whether they built it or bought it, and how they made that call. No theory, no "AI is the future" platitudes. Just the specific workflows and use cases that are actually working.

You'll learn how to:

- Identify which AI tools are worth paying for and which ones to drop
- Build AI workflows that solve real customer marketing problems, not hypothetical ones
- Make the build vs. buy decision with a clear framework instead of guessing
